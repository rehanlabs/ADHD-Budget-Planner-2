🌱 ADHD Budget Planner — Complete User Manual

A low-stimulation, privacy-first personal finance tool engineered specifically for neurodivergent minds.

1. Device Installation

iOS Safari Open https://rehanlabs.github.io/ADHD-Budget-Planner-2/ in Safari. Tap the Share button, then tap "Add to Home Screen". This protects your data from Safari's 7-day auto-clearing.
Android Chrome Open the link in Chrome. Tap the three dots (⋮) top right and tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".

2. Core Features

  • Safe to Spend Today: One single daily allowance number. Check it before making discretionary purchases.
  • Wishlist Pause (48h Vault): Park impulse shopping urges. 82% of impulses fade on their own within 48 hours.
  • Burnout Shield: A one-tap blackout emergency view for bad executive function days. Strips everything away down to one number.
  • Fixed Obligations: Payday projection and upcoming bill reserve calculation.
  • Focus Scratchpad: Zero-friction capture for money thoughts and anxiety capture.

3. Monthly Data Backups

All data stays 100% on your device in local storage. Once a month, visit Preferences → Download Backup (.json) and save the file to your Google Drive or iCloud so your budget is protected forever.
